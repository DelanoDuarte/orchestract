How a workflow works

A workflow is a graph of steps, each owned by an agent. One step is the start (where new documents begin) and at least one is an end (where the workflow completes). Transitions are named actions — like approve or request_changes — that move a document from one step to another. Add as many as you like, including loops back to earlier steps.
